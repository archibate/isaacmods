- A trip into a room bigger than the screen no longer opens on a stretch of it nobody is standing in, with or without REPENTOGON. Such a room covers several cells of the floor and has a door for each of them, and the game puts you down at the door of whichever cell the trip named. The trip named the cell you clicked, so clicking the far end of the room landed you there — and the view, which the game fixes from where it laid you and then holds for the whole fade, sat at that end until it caught up with where the mod had since carried you. The trip now names the cell the walk would have come in by, so the game picks the door and the screen itself and there is nothing left to catch up with. (thank @Whitor_大白 and @li3397450972 for report!)
- A trip further off than next door, into a room bigger than the screen, now sets out from the room the walk would have come from. Naming the cell is only half of it: the game reads which wall to come in by from the room the trip starts in, and the cell only chooses among the doors on that wall — so a target off to one side was entered from whatever wall faced you, at the wrong door and looking at the wrong part of the room. The trip steps into that last room on the way first, the same hop the mod has always made for a secret room's antechamber, and leaves from there. The REPENTOGON-only view snap is gone with it: there is no longer anything for a camera to correct. The hop only ever steps into a room already walked and cleared, so nothing is opened or paid for by taking it — which is also why a trip under AllowAnyRoom, where no path has been cleared to trace, still arrives the way it used to.
