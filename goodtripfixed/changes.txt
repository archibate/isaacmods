- The teleport map no longer disappears while a room is uncleared. It stays where it is, faint and inert, so it reads as "not right now" rather than as the mod having broken (MCM: DimMapInCombat, on by default; DimMapAlpha sets how faint).
- A trip now puts you at a door instead of wherever the game felt like. It used to work the side out from the two rooms' map positions, which reads right between neighbours and means nothing further off — a trip across the floor could leave you against a blank wall on a side you never came from. You now arrive at the door a walk would have come in by, taking the shortest way there.
- A trip no longer crosses a secret room through a wall no bomb has opened. A secret room counted as a corridor on all four of its sides, so a room reachable only past it came free, saving the bomb that opening the far side would have cost. On a run the mod joined partway, where it never got to see those walls, nothing is refused that used to be allowed.
