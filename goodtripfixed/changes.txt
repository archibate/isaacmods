- The warning about a second GoodTrip now names the mod it clashes with, instead of telling everyone to disable the old GoodTrip. What it actually notices is another mod holding the name every GoodTrip has claimed since the original, and today that is as often one of the spin-offs as the original itself, so players who had unsubscribed the original — or never had it — were sent hunting through the mod list for something that was not there. The other mod's own name is now read and printed: disable "goodtrip", or disable "GoodTrip [Fixed 2]", whichever is the one loaded beside this mod. Two of them, one loaded on each side of this mod, get a line each. A reload of this mod during a run no longer counts as a second copy of itself either. (thank @璃桦 for the report!)
