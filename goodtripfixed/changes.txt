- Fixed hand-edited settings being ignored since the last update: saving the config for everyone meant the saved copy also overrode whatever you had edited into the mod's files, and a Steam update no longer restored it. Settings now live in gtconfig.lua next to main.lua — uncomment a line there and it wins over the saved config on every launch, and the mod stops writing over it (thanks @悍匪阿博 and @空想白昼 for the reports!)
- Fixed a crash when teleporting to a secret room you had already stepped into, from an L-shaped room: the mod looked up the room you stand in by its anchor cell, and for that one shape the anchor is not part of the room, so the lookup came back empty (thanks @mata for the stack trace!)
