- With ArriveAtDoor on, a trip now lands at the walk's door on the first visit to a room too, and after a quit-and-continue. The door to stand at was looked up in what the mod had learned by standing in rooms, and from next door it had only learned that a passage exists, not which door is on the far side; a continue threw the whole record away besides. With nothing to aim at, the game's own landing stood, and the game puts you at the wall facing the room you left, by whichever of the two grid distances is larger — right only when the walk was straight. The door is now read off the map itself: the side the walk steps in by names the wall, and where the entered cell sits in a big room names which of that wall's two doors. (thank @Moe&OF for the report, and for the three-step recipe that pinned it: wrong, right after entering once, wrong again after a continue!)
