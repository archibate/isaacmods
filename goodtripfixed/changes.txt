- Fixed clicks landing two rooms away from where you aimed inside the mirror world: the mod flips the map's coordinates to match the mirrored drawing, and that flip was pinned to a fixed column, so every floor whose map did not happen to start there was off by exactly two rooms — the room under the pointer either refused the trip or sent you somewhere else. The flip now reads the map's own edges, so it lands right on any layout.
- The keyboard cursor now stops on the outermost room instead of drifting a little past it into empty space, where it selected nothing.
- New for REPENTOGON users (MCM: CursorOnGameMap): the cursor rides the game's own corner map and the mod's own window stays hidden. This is the mode withdrawn back in 2.2.38 for drawing behind the map — it needs REPENTOGON to draw above it, so without REPENTOGON the switch is not offered and does nothing.
- Fixed TAB + 1~9 bookmarking nothing at all when the keyboard cursor was not the thing aiming — right after a teleport, or with the mouse holding the map. A bookmark now takes whatever you are pointing at: the cursor when it has the aim, otherwise the mouse, and the room you stand in when neither is on the map.
- 中文玩家: 装了 Mod配置菜单（中文版）的话, 本 mod 的设置菜单现在是中文的 (原版菜单不支持中文渲染, 所以只在中文版菜单下生效).
- The settings menu is split into tabs — Map, Fairness, Shortcuts, Display, Controls, Calibration, Keybinds — so no page needs scrolling. The four calibration sliders now show their real setting names, which are the ones to write into gtconfig.lua.
