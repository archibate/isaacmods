- A trip in or out of a curse room now asks the door whether it still has its spikes, instead of asking you whether a Flat File is in your pocket. The trinket takes the spikes off the side of the door it was standing beside and they stay off, so one picked up later leaves them on and one dropped later leaves them off — neither of which is what you were being charged for. The two sides are asked about separately, which is why a trip in can be free while the trip back out still costs. (thank @Whitor_大白 for report!)
- A trip between a secret room and the room guarding it costs nothing now, either way round, when that room is the curse room. You get in there with a bomb, and the hole a bomb leaves has no spikes on it, so there was never anything to pay — the toll was being charged for a door the trip does not go through.
