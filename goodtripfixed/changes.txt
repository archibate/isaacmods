- A trip into a room a red key opened is no longer refused. The mod reads a room's walls on the way in, and the red door appears after that, so the room it bought stayed walled off for the rest of the floor. The walls of the room you stand in are now re-read as they change, which also covers a secret room bombed open and left behind for later.
- A red room that turned out to hold a shop or a treasure room is no longer refused as a special room you have to walk into. Its door stands open and the key already paid for it, so what it happens to hold is not a cost a trip is skipping.
- Red rooms draw red on the teleport map, the way the game's own map draws them. They used to be picked out by counting rooms and colouring everything past the floor's original tally, which missed any the room list had space for already.
