- A trip into a room wider or taller than the screen no longer opens on a stretch of it you are not standing in. Such a room shows one screen at a time, and the game picks which one as the room loads — going by where it laid you down, not by the door the trip is taking you to — then holds it there for the whole transition and snaps at the end. So the arrival watched an empty part of the room and jumped. You now arrive drawn back where the view still is, and the drawing comes off on the very frame the view snaps, so the two cancel and nothing stirs on screen but the room itself.
- New for REPENTOGON users (MCM: SnapViewOnLanding, on by default): the view simply follows you to the door as you land, and even that last slide of the room is gone. Without REPENTOGON there is no way to move the view at all, so the switch is not offered and does nothing. (thank @Whitor_大白 for report!)
- The teleport map now dims in an uncleared room while it is pinned, not only while TAB holds it up. The faint inert window is what the map does instead of disappearing when a room refuses a trip, and the pinned one had never been wired to it, so it still went missing mid-fight.
- Clicking a room on the pinned map no longer fires a tear along with it, the way clicking one on the held map already did not. Both are a click aimed at the map rather than at anything in front of you, but only the held one had ever been wired to the setting that says so (MCM: NoShootWhenClick).
