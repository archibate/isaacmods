- Delirium's room no longer gives itself away on the Void. Every other boss room there took a trip and that one refused, which turned a guess into a certainty. It was not a rule about Delirium at all: the map files a room under the cell it drew it on, and asked for the room's top-left corner when deciding what the pointer was over. Those are the same cell for every room the Void does not stretch across four of them, which is every room but that one. It now lights up, and takes a trip, like any other. (thank @Flowerling for report!)
- New switch in Fairness (MCM: AllowAnyRoom, off by default): a trip may go to any room on the map, walked to or not, with nothing cleared on the way. Leave it off and the trip rules stay exactly as they are.
