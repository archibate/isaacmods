- A trip into a room bigger than the screen no longer opens on a stretch of it nobody is standing in, with or without REPENTOGON. Such a room covers several cells of the floor and has a door for each of them, and the game puts you down at the door of whichever cell the trip named. The trip named the cell you clicked, so clicking the far end of the room landed you there — and the view, which the game fixes from where it laid you and then holds for the whole fade, sat at that end until it caught up with where the mod had since carried you. The trip now names the cell the walk would have come in by, so the game picks the door and the screen itself and there is nothing left to catch up with.
